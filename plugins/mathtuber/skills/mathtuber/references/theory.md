# Evidence-informed filmmaking

The full evidence library lives in the repository at docs/research (THEORY.md, SYSTEM.md, EVIDENCE.md, evidence.json). That library distinguishes experiments, reviews, correlational platform observations and design hypotheses. A bundled copy is not required to run the plugin; these operational rules are self-contained.

Aim for enjoyable mathematical discovery: attention, pleasure, comprehension, transfer, trust and return are separate outcomes. Retention does not prove learning. Agent ratings do not simulate a real audience.

Before expensive rendering, embed `creative` in project.json: audience, learning_goal, question, payoff, transfer_prompt, duration_rationale, novelty_audit, and ordered beats. Each beat names scene, viewer_question, visual_action, new_understanding, sound_intent. Run `plan-check`. It checks completeness, not quality. Check previous channel topics before claiming novelty.

Open with an understandable unresolved question in the mathematical objects themselves. Make a prediction possible before the explanation; provide enough time to think. Preserve object identity as the relationship changes. Reveal a counterexample or mechanism before naming abstract terminology. Explain why the initial guess failed. End with an earned answer and a small transfer question, not a withheld payoff.

Choose compositions per concept. A card permutation should move identifiable cards; a path reflection should visibly reflect a path. Keep labels stable and phone-readable. Motion must expose a relationship or guide attention. Do not use constant movement, a rigid cut frequency or a universal hook timer. Contrast can provide focus; sound and motion are not substitutes for reasoning.

Sound is intentional per beat: a quiet tactile cue can mark a mathematical event, an original motif can introduce/resolve a question, and silence can protect difficult reasoning. Avoid continuous decorative sound. `soundtrack` optionally supplies path, gain_db and license; assembly ducks it under narration and normalizes the mix. Declare original authorship or verify the license. Music effects vary by task and audience; no universal benefit is established.

Use evidence separately: exact computation/proof checks; inspected frames and adjacent transition samples; measured word-to-event alignment; decoded audio, independent ASR and signal checks; actual listening when supported. Record methods, coverage, limitations and audience_validation in every creative-project review. Do not infer acoustic taste from a transcript, motion quality from a single image, or learning from a model's confidence.

Prioritize fixing wrong mathematics, illegibility, misleading object movement, missing speech and timing drift before polish. After release, record watch behavior, specific misunderstandings, transfer answers and next-video behavior separately. Test one major creative hypothesis at a time across comparable videos; topic differences and recommendation traffic confound comparisons. These are production hypotheses until measured with viewers.
