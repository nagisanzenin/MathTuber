## What changed during review

The bit grid was enlarged, and its heading now acknowledges that both checks are even after correction. Gradient-descent examples now remove old trails and place intermediate numerical states on measured spoken cues. Simpson’s subgroup rectangles genuinely merge into totals; a lingering-object defect was removed before the equal-weight comparison. Fibonacci fillings keep their existing tile positions while the new final tile appears, and labels distinguish initial lengths from final-tile classes. The traffic model defines its variable explicitly and labels the old 65-minute total while the route allocation changes.

Two reusable Manim lessons: removing a wrapper group may leave members that were added separately; remove those actual members or explicitly clear and restore persistent anchors at a scene-phase boundary. Also inspect the moment a number is spoken: a mathematically correct animation can still get ahead of the explanation. A cue at a paragraph start does not synchronize every intermediate state.

## Evidence and limits

Independent arithmetic/enumeration checked all five mechanisms, including parity’s four-corner failure, the exact quadratic update multiplier, Simpson’s weighted totals, the last-tile counting bijection, and Braess equilibrium versus coordinated routing. Current final image sheets were actually opened at measured cues, declared critical intervals and the ending. These are sampled images, not continuous audiovisual viewing. Complete source ASR was read against narration; source and final audio signals were measured with no clipping. There was no subjective listening assessment. Dry narration has no music or SFX layer to judge.

The plugin test suite ran 133 tests: 130 passed, 3 skipped. Python 3.14 emitted SQLite resource warnings in tests. These unit tests do not establish artistic quality. All five final exports separately passed mechanical verification and received hash-bound reviews before publishing.

## Reflection

The batch is more varied mathematically than a geometry-led selection, with information coding, machine-learning optimization, statistical aggregation, combinatorics and network incentives. Color and typography remain consistent while the visual mechanisms differ. The strongest moments are the parity intersection, last-tile decomposition and the traffic incentive reversal: visible operations do explanatory work.

The design is still mostly schematic. Simpson begins with a dense table; the optimization clip assumes graph and slope familiarity; several explanations hold a diagram for multiple seconds. Minimalism alone is not evidence of beauty or engagement. Future changes should test opening comprehension, phone-size readability and one-step transfer with real viewers, then use retention data to locate confusion. No improvement in learning, retention or channel recognition is claimed from this production review.
