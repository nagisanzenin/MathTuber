# A relationship revealed through motion

Cycle thirteen hypothesis: let a constrained physical or geometric action build a visible trace. Retain enough of the result for comparison. The goal is a graceful explanation whose motion reveals a rule, rather than an illustrated lecture with decorative movement.

## Evidence boundaries

Monroy and Orgs (2026) tested 30 and 19 young psychology students using 6.3-second apparent-motion sequences. Smooth and synchronous sequences were generally preferred; perceived excitement and liking were distinct. Their second experiment did not find an overall liking advantage for human figures over matched abstract forms. The samples were small and predominantly female. Abruptness also changed implied velocity, and the authors acknowledge limited ecological validity. These results concern short muted movement judgments, not mathematical learning, Shorts retention or a prescription to slow every action. [Primary full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00426-025-02232-y).

Husselman and colleagues (2024) studied two samples of sixteen adults viewing street-art images. Liking increased across image groups combining greater complexity with greater conceptual fluency. These dimensions were not independently manipulated in these experiments. Extra viewing time increased liking across groups; the predicted interaction with image group was absent. EEG interpretations of flow remain indirect. This supports questioning a blanket simplicity rule, not claiming complexity causes learning or that prolonged viewing guarantees enjoyment. [Primary paper](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/324356/1/324356.pdf).

## Editorial contract

- Begin with an action and a visible constraint. A taut thread, fixed rod or rolling contact should do explanatory work.
- Preserve correspondence: the live point must meet its trace, and the trace must come from the same model.
- Retain a path or reference state when it helps comparison. Avoid an accumulating cloud of equal-weight marks.
- Use continuous motion to expose dependencies; pause at a meaningful state for a measurement or reason.
- Distinguish a geometric parameter, scene time and physical time. An eased parameter is not evidence of physical acceleration.
- Label rewinds and altered conditions. Do not silently substitute a new model.
- Make openings and endings worth observing without increasing cut frequency by rule.

Prototype one mechanism before authoring five. Compare it with a static result using the same labels and geometry. Inspect the path endpoint, tangent or linkage constraints, readable scale, useful movement and held references. A visual inspection can identify contradictions; it cannot establish viewer pleasure, calmness or learning.
