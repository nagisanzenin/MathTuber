# Focal staging and continuity

Cycle eight follows the [ending batch reflection](../endings-batch.md). Its small objects and separate representations can ask too much of the voice. The next hypothesis is to make the relevant relationship occupy the image, keep its identity through transitions, and reveal marks in time with the explanation.

## Evidence that constrains the design

Thiele and colleagues analyzed a 62-second sequence from explanation videos with 19 valid eye-tracking records after three calibration failures. Dynamic, speech-synchronized presentation directed more gaze toward the currently discussed areas than a static presentation. This exploratory study connects fine-grained audiovisual cohesion to attention; it explicitly leaves explanatory effectiveness for later work. It does not test camera zoom, portrait Shorts, calm, or channel loyalty. [Primary article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/communication/articles/10.3389/fcomm.2024.1356495/full).

A second study compared color treatments of instructional and decorative material on four hurricane web pages, in samples of 128 university and 140 secondary students. Contrasting their color treatment improved reported retention and transfer relative to uniform treatment; colorful versus monochrome design alone did not. The direction of the contrast did not significantly affect those outcomes. These were self-paced pages, not videos. Some reported test-statistic/p-value pairs and metacognitive descriptions are internally inconsistent, so we do not use their emotional or metacognitive effect estimates to justify our palette. [Primary article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1393113/full).

## Our editorial experiment, not a research result

Start close enough to recognize the subject and its behavior. Change scale only when a detail becomes the question. Move the same drawn object continuously into its explanatory position; do not quietly replace it with an unrelated diagram. Keep correspondence through position, shape, and an accent color. Attach labels to the measured feature. Show one consequential relation before adding notation. Return to a changed case the viewer can inspect.

A close view is not automatically better. A gear needs both partners visible, a capillary needs the reservoir reference level, and a ray reflection needs the incident direction. Preserve the context that makes the detail meaningful. A transition should not scale captions, crop an essential endpoint, or restart a physical process.

The implementation provides optional geometry fitting into an authored safe rectangle. It supplies no universal screen-coverage threshold, attention score, or automatic aesthetic approval. The host agent selects the subject and reviews transitions. No claim about human attention follows from a bounding box.

## Review and decision

Inspect opening recognition, the full transition, attached labels, the critical mathematical event, and the ending on the final export. Record whether the close view preserves explanatory context and whether it still feels diagrammatic in sampled evidence. Check the mathematics independently. Audio signal checks and ASR remain separate from listening.

A batch of different topics cannot isolate this staging choice causally. Keep the sources and editorial hypotheses so later audience feedback or a controlled comparison can challenge them. Do not present growing upload counts as evidence of artistic progress.
