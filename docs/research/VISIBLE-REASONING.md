# Make the relationship visible

Cycle ten hypothesis, following the [material batch reflection](../material-batch.md): an attractive object can remain an inert diagram. The next change should make an inferential step available in the image itself.

## Primary evidence

[Boucheix and Lowe (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2009.02.015) studied an interactive piano mechanism. In experiment one, 57 undergraduates learned with spreading color, arrow or absent cues. Spreading color helped local kinematics and functional mental-model scores, without improving configuration scores. In experiment two, 53 undergraduates used synchronized or persistent cues. Synchronization improved kinematics; the overall synchronization effect and functional-model main effect were not significant. The arrow treatment also differed in colors and selected locations. This supports a cautious experiment with spatially and temporally meaningful cues, not a universal rule about animation. [Full paper](https://lead.ube.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/000191-an-eye-tracking-comparison-of-external-pointing-cues-and-internal-continuous-cues-in-learning-with-complex-animations.pdf).

[Boucheix and colleagues (2013)](https://tecfa.unige.ch/tecfa/teaching/methodo/boucheix-lowe2012.pdf) randomly assigned 84 undergraduates to four versions of another three-minute, user-controlled piano animation. Two forms of relational cueing outperformed entity cueing and no cues on movement reproduction and written mental models. The two relational treatments did not differ significantly in comprehension. Initial cue engagement did not imply sustained following. The authors flag a design confound: entity cues faded other parts, while relational cues added color ribbons. There was no explanatory narration or social feed. These results do not measure peacefulness, enjoyment, artistic value or Shorts retention.

## Our inference and implementation

A reasoning beat names the relationship that becomes visible. Before animation, identify what the viewer already knows. During it, preserve the objects and reference quantities needed for comparison. After it, keep the result long enough to inspect. Narration should direct a relevant observation rather than merely state a rule while the diagram waits.

Use native Manim transformations, explicit constructions and synchronized marks. No additional service or mandatory effect is needed. A color path must not imply that force or pressure propagates slowly unless that is the modeled claim. Flat area encodings stay flat. A faded object must not become an invisible premise.

The [authoring guide](../../plugins/mathtuber/skills/mathtuber/references/visible-reasoning.md) adds a concrete before/change/after plan and a mute-image audit. It does not impose a shot template or a minimum motion count.

## Planned contrasts

- A sheared region is cut and reassembled, preserving area.
- A fixed-perimeter rectangle is related to a square and a visible deficit.
- An angle bisector links two area comparisons to a length ratio.
- Hydraulic pistons exchange force for distance through the same displaced volume.
- Oppositely traveling disturbances combine into stationary nodes.

These mechanisms do not match the reviewed archive's titles; related area, mechanics and wave themes already exist. The archive check is not a transcript audit.

## What would count against the hypothesis

Reject a treatment if the animation breaks object correspondence, hides a needed premise, or asks viewers to compare too many simultaneous changes. Reduce it if the same relationship is clearer in a held diagram. Test the exact transition in captioned previews before full rendering. Final image and transcript inspection remain editorial evidence, not viewer testing. A later audience study should separately examine recognition, explanation of the relationship, enjoyment and voluntary continuation.
