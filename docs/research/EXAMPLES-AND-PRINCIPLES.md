# From an example to the relationship it carries

Cycle fifteen broadened the mathematics, but several films ended as sparse formula slides. The next experiment is to preserve the meaning of the example as its notation changes. This is an editorial experiment, not a demonstrated audience effect.

## Evidence and limits

[Liao (2023), Mapping principles and worked examples for structural learning](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1241873/full) analyzed 138 undergraduates randomly assigned to five printed-material conditions involving geometric and Poisson distributions. Each principle had 35 minutes of study and mapping, followed by a 20-minute test allocation. Methods, results and discussion were read; supplements were not.

Mapping a principle to an example elicited more relational comparisons for the complex principle, but not for the simple principle. Most comparisons of conceptual and structural outcomes did not show significant differences between principle–example and example–example mapping. This is not proof of equivalence or a universal advantage. The full-sample conceptual-test reliability was nearly zero; an analysis excluding unconventional response patterns produced similar findings. Learning performance was limited, and participants could avoid difficult correspondences. The experiment did not test narrated video, Shorts retention, aesthetics or anxiety reduction.

Our earlier [recognition research](RECOGNITION-AND-LISTENING.md) and [context research](CONTEXT-AND-ABSTRACTION.md) also caution against treating concrete detail as an automatic improvement.

## Production experiment

Choose one correspondence that makes the argument possible. In a remainder film, it is the count and its two remainders; in an image-filter film, it is the same neighboring intensities and their difference. Show what each symbol retains from the example before relying on the general statement.

Preserve identified objects while regrouping or simplifying. Use labels and position as well as color. Explicitly distinguish the worked case, the reason the relationship holds, and its range of validity. A successful example alone is not a proof. A named theorem without a visible argument is not a payoff.

Prototype the complete example-to-rule transition in one film before expanding the batch. Inspect the point where a novice could lose track of what a quantity means, including the ending. Avoid turning this into a mandatory dashboard or a formula quota. Mathematical value determines the representation.

## Renderer lesson from cycle fifteen

A hidden `always_redraw` readout can stop updating after `FadeOut` removes it from the scene. If its tracker changes while it is absent, `FadeIn` can temporarily show an obsolete value. Refresh the mobject recursively with `update(0)` before fading it back in, or construct a fresh readout from current state. Check the first visible frames after reappearance, not merely the settled endpoint.

This repair prevented a prediction pointer from showing four while its reappearing labels still described three. The same principle applies to denominators, counts, units and bounds. Rendering success is insufficient evidence of mathematical consistency.
