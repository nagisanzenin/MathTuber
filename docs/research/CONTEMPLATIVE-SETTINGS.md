# Continuous settings, legible captions, and aesthetic restraint

This cycle responds to [the events batch](../events-batch.md): the rounded triangle carried its explanation more naturally than the sticker film's abrupt recurrence. Generic captions also conflicted with the channel's typography. These are editorial observations from sampled media, not audience measurements.

## Research and limits

[Désiron and Schneider (2024)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1393113/full) investigate colorfulness and information relevance in multimedia documents. Their findings distinguish cognitive load, metacognitive judgments and emotional engagement. This is adjacent evidence for using color to establish relevance, not proof that our warm-paper palette improves mathematical understanding. We will give semantic objects the visual emphasis and avoid adding unrelated atmospheric decoration.

[The Chinese poetry study (2021)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.621969/full) is particularly relevant to the wish to combine insight and appreciation. Its formal experiment involved 166 adolescents and manipulated prior emotion induction and colorful learning material. Retention and transfer showed effects, but emotional, aesthetic and motivational outcomes did not move uniformly; the formal experiment found no main effect of internal colorfulness on aesthetic preference and no motivation effects. Only one pastoral poem was studied. We cannot transfer these results to math Shorts or infer that peaceful design reduces anxiety. Evaluate appeal, comprehension and emotional tone separately.

[Gerber-Morón and Szarkowska's subtitle line-break study](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10051115/1/Line%20breaks%20in%20subtitling_AfterReview_APA_FINAL.pdf), discussed in the previous supplement, motivates a practical repair: agents may choose line boundaries within exact spoken phrases. This does not make a character-count heuristic a validated reading model. Inspect the rendered captions at phone size; font metrics, language and background matter.

## Production hypotheses

- One scene can be an environment for thinking: a spreading cloud, a film junction, a drop, a drawn curve, or a globe. Keep the relevant object while explaining it.
- Let structure supply beauty. Color a light ray to identify its path; trace an interpolation because it constructs a curve. Neither motion needs an unrelated flourish.
- Use a quiet opening with a visible change worth noticing. The explanation should answer the observation rather than introduce a separate quiz.
- Make the difficult inference perceptible before summarizing it symbolically. A return loop should return; a force balance should visibly close; shared interpolation fractions should move together.
- Freeze the caption phrases and composition after prototype review. Late changes invalidate evidence and consume time; caching cannot fix unstable authorship.

## Implemented support

Profile 0.6 adds validated caption typography, color, outline, size and margins. Project overrides allow a dark scene to use a suitable caption color. Old profiles retain legacy defaults. Fonts are installed dependencies, not bundled assets; renderer fallback must be inspected on each platform. Explicit line boundaries preserve the authored words and the original timing span, with at most two bounded lines. No typography setting is an automatic readability or taste score.

The next batch tests these choices across five mathematical settings. Mathematical checks remain independent of visual appeal. Full audio decoding and ASR do not replace subjective listening; sampled images do not replace an uninterrupted viewing session. No engagement gain is claimed in advance.
