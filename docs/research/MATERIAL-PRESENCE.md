# Material presence and explanatory simplicity

Cycle eight's subjects are easier to inspect at larger size, but many still resemble marks on a page. Cycle nine tests restrained material shading on tangible objects while keeping mathematical annotations flat. This is an editorial hypothesis, not an established improvement in Shorts quality.

## Evidence

Höst, Schönborn and Tibell (2022) surveyed 94 adult respondents choosing between biological images at a science center. Across seventeen images, perceived realism correlated with stated desire to explore, but general preference did not follow the same pattern. Shadow coding predicted exploration positively and preference negatively in the authors' models. The stated exploration ranking did not correlate with earlier exhibit-use rankings. Subjects and visual features varied together; this was not a randomized same-object shading intervention. It measured neither learning nor Shorts retention. [Primary study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2022.933087/full).

Huey and colleagues found that explanatory drawings emphasized functional parts and symbols. Their consequences differed by task: explanations supported action selection but made object identification harder; emphasizing causal parts did not guarantee easier part identification. This motivates separate recognition and mechanism checks. It does not test our rendering style. The author manuscript's chance-level wording is awkward beside its raw percentages; we do not import those numerical estimates. [Published abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36870147/), [author manuscript, methods and results inspected](https://cogtoolslab.github.io/pdf/huey_psyarxiv_2021.pdf).

## Experiment

Use profile-colored nested vector washes on a tangible round object. The outline stays exact and the subject can translate without changing its identity. The wash is a painted depth cue, not an irradiance model. Keep area, probability and mathematical point encodings visually distinct. A mark indicating physical rotation may rotate independently of the apparent light direction.

A rendered flat-versus-painted bob comparison was inspected before implementation. Geometry checks verify that all layers remain within the circular outline, translation/scaling preserves its dimensions, and invalid radii/layer counts fail. These checks establish implementation behavior, not artistic success.

For each film, inspect: whether the object reads as the intended thing; whether its action and consequence remain visible; whether labels retain contrast; whether depth implies a scientifically false geometry; and whether the opening begins cleanly without reintroducing an already visible object. Review the ending as a changed case, and record remaining weaknesses without an invented quality score.

The aim is quiet material character and an invitation to look closer. More texture, realism or rendering cost is not itself progress.
