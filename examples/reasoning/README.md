# Visible reasoning studies

Five original films use area rearrangement, a geometric-mean semicircle construction, two views of an area ratio, hydraulic volume accounting, and wave superposition. [Research and limitations](../../docs/research/VISIBLE-REASONING.md) explain the design hypothesis.

Each project includes a manifest, editable scenes, cue/review assets, an explicit before/change/preserved/after reasoning plan, and mathematical verification. Initialize a project from its manifest, copy its scenes and assets, synthesize local narration, compile cues, render a captioned preview, and inspect it before the final render. The entire batch needs current final review before upload.

Geometric transformations preserve exact relationships. Hydraulic diagrams ignore friction and height-pressure differences; colored swept regions count volume rather than trace water parcels. Separate wave rows are mathematical contributions and their sum; the model is linear and presented slowly. Uniform drawing scale is not a physical unit conversion claim.

The work remains an editorial experiment. Image sampling, numerical checks and transcription do not establish enjoyment, learning, peacefulness or retention. Credentials, publishing intents, media and private state are excluded.
