# Physical context studies

Five original vector films connect recognizable subjects to mathematical representations: a river boat and relative velocity; a candle camera and projection; a hill and its height gradient; a siphon and gravitational head; a rope and limiting friction.

[Research and limitations](../../docs/research/CONTEXT-AND-ABSTRACTION.md) distinguish the editorial hypothesis from controlled learning evidence. Each editable project contains a manifest, authored scenes, cue/review assets, a context and novelty plan, and independent mathematical checks. Initialize a project, copy its scenes/assets, synthesize local narration, compile cues, render and inspect a captioned preview, then render and review the final output. The whole batch requires current final review before publishing.

Physical drawings are illustrative; the documented ideal models govern the measured relationships. Image sampling and transcription do not establish aesthetic preference or learning. Credentials, publishing intents, private state and media are excluded.
