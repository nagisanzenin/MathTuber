# Mathematical objects with material presence

Five original scenes explore pendulum length, rolling mass distribution, thermal expansion of a hole, rebound height, and springs in series and parallel. [Research and scope](../../docs/research/MATERIAL-PRESENCE.md) distinguish artistic depth cues from scientific encoding.

Initialize each manifest, copy scenes and JSON assets, synthesize narration and compile cues. Inspect captioned previews before final renders. Review every cue, declared critical intervals and the actual ending. The whole batch must have current final reviews before upload.

The pendulum uses a small-angle ideal model. Rolling objects are shown from the side and require sufficient static friction. Thermal expansion is uniform and exaggerated. The rebound model covers a single impact with chosen restitution and no drag. Spring transitions interpolate settled positions rather than simulating dynamics. Mathematical checks accompany each project.

Painted shading is illustrative, not an irradiance model. Mathematical annotations stay flat. Sampled frames and transcription do not establish enjoyment, calmness, natural prosody, learning or retention. Credentials, media, private state and publishing intents are excluded.
