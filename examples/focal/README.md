# A closer view of a mathematical relationship

Five original scenes follow capillary rise, a parabolic mirror, single-slit diffraction, rotating weights and gear ratios. Their staging preserves subject identity through an enlarged view. See [focal staging research](../../docs/research/FOCAL-STAGING.md).

Initialize each manifest as a project, copy its scenes and JSON assets, synthesize narration and compile cues and sound events. Inspect captioned prototypes before final rendering and assembly. Review the final opening, every cue, all declared critical intervals and the actual ending. The batch gate requires current final reviews for all five before the first upload.

Capillary widths are exaggerated and transient flow is not simulated. The mirror uses ideal geometric rays. Diffraction brightness is compressed and its peak normalized; horizontal position represents sine angle. Rotating weights include a nonzero platform inertia and require internal work to move inward. Gear teeth are schematic and their exact rotation ratio assumes proper engagement. Independent numerical and analytic checks are supplied.

Dry narration and sampled visual evidence do not establish pleasant listening, perceived calm, retention or learning. Media, credentials and publication intents are excluded.
