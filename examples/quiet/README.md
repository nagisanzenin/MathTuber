# Five quiet mathematical relationships

Ellipse reflection, circular projection and springs, the volume of a ring, circle inversion, and lunar phases. Each source is original programmatic geometry with a pinned warm-paper profile and local narration. The Moon uses a dark viewing panel within the same restrained palette.

These films test optional 1.1-second pauses between authored paragraphs. That duration is an editorial hypothesis, not an experimentally established optimum. See [quiet-beat research](../../docs/research/QUIET-BEATS.md).

Initialize a project from each manifest, copy scenes and assets, synthesize speech, compile literal cues and sound events, and inspect captioned prototypes before final rendering. Regenerate timing after changing narration. Media, model weights, private publication records and credentials are excluded; fonts are separately licensed dependencies.

The independent math-verification files describe assumptions and numerical checks. Read the source conditions as well: ideal geometric reflection, a linear undamped spring, a rotation axis outside the disk, inversion away from its center, and a geometric illuminated-area model of the Moon. The lunar narration explicitly distinguishes ordinary phases from eclipses and includes orbital tilt.

Preview revisions fixed ambiguous speech cues, text crossing a construction, a reciprocal-distance example left visible at the unit circle, a premature ring surface before its sweep, renderer edge cases and animation overruns. Final inspection is a separate gate. Sampled images and ASR do not establish continuous audiovisual viewing, pleasing prosody or audience outcomes.
