# Continuous geometric construction

Five original vector films make a curve through a visible constraint: an unwinding tangent thread, perpendicular sliders, a copying linkage, a fixed-length follower and a ray-wise offset. Each construction pauses at the relationship that explains the trace, then resumes with a changed condition.

[Research and limits](../../docs/research/CONTINUOUS-CONSTRUCTION.md) distinguish aesthetic experiments from this editorial hypothesis. Initialize a project from its manifest, copy scenes/assets, synthesize narration, compile cues, render a captioned preview, then inspect the final output before publishing. CurveTrace caches a geometric path; its parameter is not arc length or a claim about physical speed. Timing assets are reference outputs and must be recompiled after narration changes.

Independent mathematical checks and model assumptions accompany each project. Sampled visual inspection and transcription do not establish artistic preference, natural prosody or audience learning. Credentials, publishing intents, private state and media are excluded.
