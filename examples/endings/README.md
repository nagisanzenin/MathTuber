# A changed condition, a resolved ending

Five original films follow fountain angles, lantern illumination, filling vessels, a moving pulley, and a falling-leaf model. Each final section changes a meaningful condition and shows its consequence. See [endings and curiosity research](../../docs/research/ENDINGS-AND-CURIOSITY.md).

Initialize a project from its manifest, copy scenes and JSON assets, synthesize narration, compile cues and sound events, then render a captioned preview. Inspect it before final rendering and assembly. The empty sound-event lists deliberately produce dry narration. After assembly, run the plugin's `ending_preview.py --project PROJECT_PATH` and inspect its export-bound ending sheet alongside distributed frames, every cue, and the declared critical intervals. Review all five final exports before publishing any of the batch.

The fountain assumes equal launch speed and equal endpoint heights without drag; changing its angle shows steady trajectories rather than a physical nozzle transient. Lantern shading represents energy per area, not perceptual brightness. The conical and cylindrical vessels share capacity and height. The ideal slow pulley lift excludes friction and wheel weight. The leaf uses fixed orientation and quadratic drag; it does not simulate flutter. Mathematical derivations and numeric checks are included separately from scene code.

Media, credentials and upload intents are excluded. These sources do not establish viewer learning, calm, engagement, or satisfaction. A complete narrated answer is not evidence of a learner-generated explanation.
